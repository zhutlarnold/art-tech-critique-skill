# 艺术批判工业与科技 / Art Critiquing Industry and Technology

中英文项目说明 / Bilingual project statement · 2026-10-06 (Asia/Shanghai)

## 项目目标 / Project purpose

从艺术形式与真实作品图出发，分析作品怎样讨论工业、科技和社会关系，并以自然文字引出有依据的生活哲思。

Analyse how artworks address industry, technology and social relations through visual form and sourced evidence, then connect the analysis to everyday ethical or philosophical questions in natural language.

## 功能与方法 / Capabilities and method

视觉选题筛选、艺术与技术关联、作者意图区分、真实配图、自然改稿、生活讨论、证据验收、查重与历史登记。

Visual topic screening, art/technology analysis, intent separation, source imagery, natural revision, everyday discussion, evidence review, deduplication and persistent history.

这是复用研究、分析与交付流程的Skill，不是训练出的专用模型。公开相关项目的方法已有重合，项目差异在具体任务组合、证据规则与迭代要求，不宣称市场首创或零相似。

This is a reusable research, analysis and delivery workflow, not a specially trained model. Related public workflows overlap with parts of the method; the project’s distinction lies in its task combination, evidence rules and iterative requirements, not an unverified claim of market originality.

## 项目发起者贡献 / Project initiator’s contribution

提出艺术批判科技与工业的方向；要求文本自然、读者可理解且联系生活；指出盐圈图像缺少吸引力，推动增加有画面依据的视觉筛选；要求版本留痕及跨Skill素材限制。

Defined the art-critique direction, required natural and accessible writing linked to everyday life, identified weak visual appeal in the salt-circle example, and requested evidence-based visual screening, version traceability and shared media restrictions.

上述角色依据本项目需求与反馈过程整理，未冒称独立编写全部代码。AI辅助完成网络资料整理、草稿、文件与脚本实现、打包和工具检查；项目发起者负责方向、评审标准和反馈决策。正式用于申请时应按自己的实际参与情况表达，不把AI参与抹去。

These roles are documented from the project’s requirements and feedback history; they do not imply sole authorship of all code. AI assistance supported source synthesis, drafts, file/script implementation, packaging and tool-based checks. The initiator supplied direction, review criteria and feedback decisions. An application statement should reflect actual participation and retain this attribution.

## 可检查产出 / Inspectable evidence

《Oil》与《ImageNet Roulette》迭代案例；盐圈案例的实际使用反馈促成视觉筛选修订。出处与验收范围见现有案例复盘、VALIDATION与CHANGELOG。

Revised Oil and ImageNet Roulette cases informed the workflow. Feedback on the salt-circle case prompted stronger visual selection. Sources and validation scope remain in the existing lessons, VALIDATION and CHANGELOG.
